Keeping dozens of tabs open or losing important links inside endless bookmark folders slows down your daily workflow.

That's why we built **Teamspaces Online for Chrome**: a fast, lightweight extension that keeps the links you use every day organized in **spaces and sections**, one click away.

---

## What you can do with the extension

### Spaces, sections, and new tab page
Group your quick links by project, client, or topic. Drag to reorder, rename, or archive spaces when a project wraps up, and access everything every time you open a new tab.

![Full page view of spaces and sections in Teamspaces Online for Chrome](/images/blog/extension/full-page-light.png)

### Quick search (`Alt+Shift+S`)
Open the popup and start typing to search the quick links of every space. Press `Enter` to open the first result right away.

![Quick search across all spaces](/images/blog/extension/quick-search.png)

### Save the current tab in one click
Pick the space and section and press `+`. You can also right-click any page or link (*"Save page/link to Teamspaces Online"*) or press `Alt+Shift+D`.

![Teamspaces Online popup to save the current tab](/images/blog/extension/popup-save-tab.png)

### Light and dark theme
Switch seamlessly to match your setup at any time.

![Dark theme in Teamspaces Online for Chrome](/images/blog/extension/full-page-dark.png)

---

## Private by design

Everything is stored **locally in your browser** (IndexedDB). There is no account, no sign-up, and no server: your links never leave your computer.

The only external request is fetching the favicon of each link from Google's favicon service (`www.google.com/s2/favicons`) using only the link's domain, never the full URL.
